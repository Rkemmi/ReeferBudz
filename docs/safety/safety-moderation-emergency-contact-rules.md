# ReeferBudz Safety, Moderation, and Emergency Contact Rules

Status: Final
Date: August 7, 2026

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the operating rules for:

- safety positioning
- user boundaries
- reporting
- blocking
- moderation
- emergency-contact feature rules
- sensitive safety-data handling
- minimum protections required before deeper social features expand

Its purpose is to keep ReeferBudz safety-related product decisions clear, consistent, and aligned with the platform’s actual capabilities.

## 2. Core Safety Position

ReeferBudz is an adults-only friendship and community platform. Because of that, it must treat safety, privacy, boundaries, and reporting as core operating concerns.

ReeferBudz can support more structured and privacy-conscious interaction, but it must not claim that it can eliminate misuse, prevent all harm, or guarantee user safety.

ReeferBudz must not:

- promise guaranteed safety
- imply that all members are verified if they are not
- imply that harmful behavior is impossible on the platform
- present moderation as stronger than it actually is
- collect sensitive safety information without a documented purpose

## 3. Safety Role in the Product

Safety in ReeferBudz should include:

- adults-only access requirements
- clear boundaries for acceptable behavior
- privacy-conscious visibility controls
- reporting options
- blocking options
- moderation review structure
- careful handling of sensitive information
- accurate language about what the platform does and does not do

Safety should be visible, understandable, and supported by product behavior.

Safety should not be used as decorative language meant to make the platform sound more protective than it actually is.

## 4. Community Boundary Position

ReeferBudz is a friendship- and community-first platform.

Because of that, it must maintain clear boundaries against:

- dating-app positioning
- hookup-first behavior
- cannabis sales or delivery behavior
- unsafe meetup culture
- coercive, threatening, or manipulative behavior
- harassment
- impersonation
- exploitative use of personal information

These boundaries should appear in both public-facing language and actual product enforcement rules.

## 5. Reporting Rules

### Reporting Must Exist Before Expansion

Before deeper social features expand, ReeferBudz must define a clear reporting path for inappropriate, unsafe, abusive, misleading, or boundary-violating behavior.

### Reporting Should Cover

- harassment
- impersonation
- threats
- coercive behavior
- inappropriate sexual behavior
- unsafe meetup conduct reported through the platform
- misleading profile behavior
- community-guideline violations
- prohibited commerce or transaction behavior
- other misuse that violates platform rules

### Reporting Standard

Reporting should:

- be easy to find
- be easy to understand
- avoid requiring users to guess what qualifies
- avoid promising outcomes that moderation cannot guarantee

## 6. Blocking Rules

Blocking should be treated as a core boundary tool, not an optional extra.

Blocking should allow a member to reduce or end unwanted interaction with another member in line with the real capabilities of the platform.

Blocking rules should define:

- what a block does
- what a block prevents
- what remains visible or inaccessible after a block
- whether block effects apply to discovery, future messaging, connections, or profile visibility

ReeferBudz must not describe blocking vaguely. Members should not have to guess what a block actually changes.

## 7. Moderation Rules

### Moderation Position

Moderation should be presented as a review and enforcement function, not as a guarantee of prevention.

### Moderation Must Define

- what types of behavior can trigger review
- what records are created during review
- who can access moderation records
- what enforcement actions are available
- what repeat or serious violations can lead to
- how non-public moderation data is separated from profile data

### Moderation Language Rule

ReeferBudz should not imply that all harmful behavior will be caught before it happens.

It can say:

- moderation exists
- reports are reviewed
- prohibited behavior can lead to enforcement action

It should not say:

- all reports are resolved instantly
- all bad actors are screened out in advance
- the platform can guarantee safe outcomes

## 8. Minimum Safety Protections Before Deeper Social Features

Before ReeferBudz expands into messaging, live activity, or more direct member-to-member interaction, it must define at minimum:

- reporting rules
- blocking rules
- moderation rules
- privacy visibility rules
- account-boundary rules
- sensitive-data handling rules
- accurate safety language

Without those rules, deeper interaction features increase product ambiguity and risk.

## 9. Emergency Contact Feature Position

### Current Position

Guardian is founder-directed as a planned, user-started, time-limited safety-session feature. Its v1 product flow, consent baseline, alert timeline, restricted-data model, retention baseline, and production gates are specified in `docs/product/guardian-v1-product-spec.md`.

This planning decision does not make Guardian operational. Emergency-contact collection, contact verification, active-session location sharing, alert delivery, audit records, deletion controls, native mobile behavior, and production readiness are not yet implemented.

### Product Caution

Emergency-contact functionality can create strong user expectations. Because of that, ReeferBudz must define the feature carefully before launch.

The platform must not imply that an emergency-contact feature creates live monitoring, emergency response, welfare checks, or guaranteed intervention unless those capacities actually exist and are intentionally supported.

### Emergency Contact Feature Must Define

- what information is collected
- why it is collected
- whether the feature is optional or required
- when the information is used
- who can access it
- what user action makes it relevant
- how it is updated or removed
- whether it creates any real operational obligation for the platform

## 10. Emergency Contact Data Rules

Emergency-contact information must be treated as restricted safety data.

It must not be treated as standard profile information.

Emergency-contact data must have:

- a documented collection purpose
- a documented access rule
- a documented retention rule
- a documented update and removal rule
- a documented use limitation

Emergency-contact information must not be visible to other members.

Basic account creation must not require an emergency contact or location permission. A verified contact and active-session location permission may be required before a member starts Guardian.

## 11. Privacy and Safety Language Rules

ReeferBudz can say:

- safety-conscious
- privacy-conscious
- reporting tools
- blocking tools
- visible safety awareness
- member visibility controls
- community-guidelines enforcement

ReeferBudz should not say, unless the product truly supports it:

- guaranteed safety
- emergency response service
- verified-safe members
- real-time intervention
- constant monitoring
- fully protected community

## 12. Sensitive Safety Data Rules

Sensitive safety data includes:

- emergency-contact information
- reporting records
- moderation records
- block records
- internal trust-and-safety notes
- review or enforcement records

This information must be:

- access-limited
- separated from normal profile visibility
- unavailable to other members
- handled as operational data, not social data

## 13. Safety and Moderation Operational Standards

ReeferBudz should operate from these standards:

- define boundaries before expanding higher-risk features
- keep safety language accurate
- separate visible profile data from restricted safety records
- avoid promising outcomes the platform cannot guarantee
- make reporting and blocking understandable
- document moderation access and enforcement rules before launch of deeper social features
- treat emergency-contact information as sensitive infrastructure, not as a casual add-on

## 14. Safety, Moderation, and Emergency Contact Do's

ReeferBudz should:

- keep safety language accurate
- make boundaries visible
- support reporting before deeper interaction expands
- support blocking before deeper interaction expands
- document moderation rules clearly
- keep emergency-contact information restricted
- separate safety operations from public-facing brand language

## 15. Safety, Moderation, and Emergency Contact Don'ts

ReeferBudz must not:

- promise guaranteed safety
- overstate moderation capabilities
- imply real-time emergency support if none exists
- collect emergency-contact data without a documented use case
- expose safety records to other members
- launch higher-risk social features without reporting and blocking rules
- use safety language as marketing exaggeration

## 16. Short Safety System Statement

ReeferBudz uses a safety-conscious platform model built around clear community boundaries, privacy-aware controls, reporting and blocking rules, moderation structure, and restricted handling of sensitive safety information, including any future emergency-contact data.
